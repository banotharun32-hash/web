Our Philosophy

Authentic Flavors, Royal Grace

“Priya Restaurant brings together authentic flavors, modern presentation, warm hospitality, and a comfortable dining experience. Our goal is to make every visit memorable through delicious food, quality ingredients, and excellent service.”

Founded in Hyderabad, Priya Restaurant was born from a singular passion: to resurrect the noble dining rituals of royal durbars and modern South Indian culinary artistry. We honor our heritage by grinding whole spices fresh each dawn, aging our basmati rice in cool cedar chambers, and preparing each dum pot sealed with fresh wheat dough.

From celebratory family dinners to corporate banquet summits, our team greets each patron with warmth, ensuring every feast is unforgettable.
